A Letter I've Been
Waiting to Write

My dearest Mehul,

There are no words big enough to describe what you mean to me. But I'll try — because you deserve every letter, every syllable, every breath of this.

You are the reason I feel safe in this world. When everything feels too loud, too heavy, too overwhelming — you are my quiet. You are the one place I can go and feel like everything is going to be okay. Not because you fix things. But because you make me feel held, seen, and completely loved.

I love the way you care. Not loudly — but in those small, breathtaking moments no one else would notice. The way you check on me. The way you just know when I need you before I even say a word. The way you love me without asking me to earn it. That is rare. You are rare.

You are my comfort when the world feels cold. You are the warmth I run to every time. You are the person whose voice alone makes my whole chest feel lighter. And I say this with every part of me — you healed parts of me I didn't even know were broken.

I think about every late night, every soft moment, every time you looked at me and made me feel like I was the only person in the world that mattered. Those moments are not small to me. They are everything. You are everything. And I would collect every single one of them forever if I could.

So today, and every single day after this, I want you to know: being loved by you is the greatest gift I have ever received. And I plan to treasure it — and you — for as long as I breathe. I love you more than I will ever be able to say, Mehul. More than any letter. More than any word.

Forever yours, Sahil ❤️

Why I Need You
More Than Words

01

The Way You Check On Me

You have this quiet habit of checking on me — not in a loud, dramatic way, but in the softest, most gentle way. A simple "are you okay?" from you carries more weight than an entire conversation with anyone else. You see through me. You always know when something is wrong before I even open my mouth. And every time you ask, every time you reach out — I feel chosen. I feel seen. I feel like I matter to someone in this world in the most irreplaceable way.

02

The Calm You Give Me

There is a very specific kind of peace that only exists around you. When my mind is spiraling, when the noise gets too loud, when I feel like I'm slowly coming undone — I think of you, and something inside me breathes again. You are the stillness inside the storm. You are the one place in this entire world where I can fall apart completely and still feel completely safe. That kind of calm is not found. It's gifted. And you give it to me every single day without even realizing it.

03

The Love You Don't Even Notice

The most beautiful thing about the way you love me is that you don't perform it — you just live it. It's in the small things. The way you remember things I've said in passing. The way you make sure I've eaten. The way you stay up just so I don't feel alone. Most people wouldn't even notice these moments. But I do. I notice every single one of them. And I collect them like treasure because each one tells me — without a single word — that I am loved by someone extraordinary.

04

The Safety of Your Arms

I never quite understood what people meant when they said someone felt like home — until I found you. Being with you feels like finally arriving somewhere after a very long and exhausting journey. There is no pretending with you. No armour, no guards, no performance. Just me — raw and real and completely at ease. In your arms, the world shrinks down to something manageable. In your warmth, I remember that I am okay. That everything is going to be okay. Because you are here. And that is always enough.

♥ from my soul to yours

I used to think love was supposed to feel urgent and desperate — like something you had to fight for every single day. But loving you has taught me something different. Loving you feels like exhaling. It feels like taking off something heavy I didn't even know I was carrying. You didn't sweep me off my feet. You quietly, gently, and completely became the ground I stand on.

There are people in this world who make you feel good when things are going well. And then there are the rare, extraordinary ones who make you feel safe even when everything is falling apart. You are the second kind. You hold my worst days with the same tenderness as my best ones. You never made me feel like too much. You never flinched at my mess. You just stayed — and staying, I've learned, is the most profound declaration of love there is.

I think about what my life looked like before you found your way into it — and it feels like trying to remember a dream I've mostly forgotten. You have become so woven into who I am that I genuinely cannot imagine the texture of my days without you in them. Your voice. Your care. The way you make the ordinary feel sacred. These things are not small. They are everything. You are everything.

On the days I forget my own worth — and there are more of those days than I ever let on — you remind me. Not with grand speeches, but with the quiet certainty of someone who sees me completely and still chooses to stay. That is the greatest gift you have ever given me: the ability to see myself through your eyes, even just for a moment, and believe that I am someone worth loving.

So here is my promise to you, Mehul — written not in ink, but in every heartbeat that belongs to you. I will love you in the ordinary moments and the impossible ones. I will show up for you the way you have always shown up for me. I will spend every day trying to be for you what you have always been for me: a safe place. A soft landing. A home that never asks you to be anything other than exactly, perfectly yourself. — Sahil, who loves you more than words.
